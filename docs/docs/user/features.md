# Features

Nexara bundles the tools a small community needs into one application. Every
item is scoped to your community, and most can be limited to a subgroup or kept
private.

## Posts and the activity feed

Members share posts written in Markdown, with images, GIFs, and links. Others
can reply in threaded comments and react with emoji. The feed shows what is new
across the community, and you can mute or block users to tailor what you see.

## Events and calendar

Create one-off or recurring events, set a location, and collect RSVPs. Hosts
can add custom RSVP labels and contribution slots (for example "bring a salad").
The calendar view shows everything you can see, and events can be exported as
iCal to your own calendar app.

## Chat

Real-time group and direct messaging runs over WebSockets. Subgroups get their
own channels, and unread counts and notifications follow your preferences.

## Galleries

Galleries are named albums with bulk upload. Photos and videos are optimized for
the web in the background. Each album has its own access control, so a family
album can stay separate from a club album.

## Subgroups

Subgroups partition a community, for example a team, a household, or a
committee. They have their own feed, events, and chat channels, and only
members of the subgroup can see content shared with it.

## Visibility

Posts and events use one of three levels:

| Level | Who can see it |
| ----- | -------------- |
| Community | All members |
| Subgroup | Members of the chosen subgroup |
| Private | Only the author |

## Polls, search, and notifications

Polls support single choice, multiple choice, yes/no, and photo options with
live results. Full-text search covers all content you are allowed to view.
Notifications arrive in-app, as Web Push, and by email, and each channel can be
tuned in your settings.

## Installable app

Nexara is a progressive web app. Install it from your browser to get an
app-like experience with an offline shell and push notifications.
