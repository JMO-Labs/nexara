# Lists

## Overview

Lists let your community keep shared checklists and signup sheets in one place: packing lists, potluck dishes, volunteer rotas, chore charts.

Every list has a **visibility**:

| Visibility  | Who can see it                                      |
|-------------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| `community` | All members of the community                        |
| `subgroup`  | Only members of the chosen subgroup                 |
| `private`   | Only you (the author)                               |

## Creating a list

1. Open **Lists** from the navigation.
2. Select **New list**.
3. Enter a title and an optional description.
4. Choose a visibility. For `subgroup`, also pick the subgroup.
5. Select **Create**.

!!! note
    You can only choose a subgroup you belong to.

## Templates

Templates save you from retyping lists you reuse, such as "Camping trip".

**Start a list from a template**

1. Select **New list**, then **From template**.
2. Pick a template. Its items are copied into your new list.
3. Adjust the title, visibility and items, then select **Create**.

**Save a template**

1. Open a list you want to reuse.
2. Select **Save as template** and give it a name.

## Items

**Add an item**

1. Open the list.
2. Type the item in the **Add item** field and press Enter.

**Complete an item**

1. Tick the checkbox next to the item (or focus it and press Space).
2. Untick it to mark it as not done.

**Edit or remove an item**

1. Open the item menu and choose **Edit** or **Delete**.

## Signups

Items can be set to accept signups, so members can volunteer for them (for example, "Bring dessert").

**Claim an item**

1. Open the list and find an item that accepts signups.
2. Select **Claim**. Your name appears on the item.

**Release an item**

1. Find the item you claimed.
2. Select **Release**. The item is open for others again.

!!! tip
    Release an item as early as you can so someone else can take it.

## Notifications

You are notified when something changes on lists you can see, such as an item you claimed being edited or a list being shared with you. Notifications arrive in-app, by Web Push and by email, depending on your settings.

**Change your preferences**

1. Open **Settings**, then **Notifications**.
2. Switch each channel (in-app, push, email) on or off for list notifications.
3. Save.
